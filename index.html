Screening Is a Starting Point, Not a Diagnosis.

MindPark's parent-guided screening is designed to help you notice patterns and early signs associated with autism and ADHD. It is a screening and evaluation tool, not a medical diagnosis. Use the information as a starting point for a more informed conversation with your child's healthcare professional.
